Loading state · from 11c

The mark, loading

Three loops, all built from the 11c geometry and all obeying the VI motion rule: nothing rotates, nothing bounces, and the dash pattern never marches. The loading feeling comes from sequence around the hexagon instead. Ids La–Lc.

La Relay

Each spoke and its node light in turn, clockwise. The hub and cage never move, so the mark stays legible the whole loop — the safest default.

Lb Reach out

The VI's brand-moment motion put on a loop: spokes land outward from the hub, nodes arrive behind them, everything clears, repeat. Most expressive — best for a splash, not a button.

Lc Minimal · inline

Structure drops back to a ghost and only six equal dots travel the cage. The one that still reads at 20px — this is the in-button and pull-to-refresh spinner.
