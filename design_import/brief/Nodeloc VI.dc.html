NODELOC

One node, then another, then a community.

The mark is a hub inside a cage: a solid centre, and six dashed reaches out to the members around it. Everything in this identity follows from that one drawing.

01 · The mark

Construction

A regular hexagon on a 40-unit radius, drawn on a 130×130 grid centred at 65,65. Six equal 40-unit sides mean six identical spokes, so one dash rhythm serves the whole mark.

Grid

Cage vertices on R40 at 30°, 90°, 150°, 210°, 270°, 330°. Hub is R12. Spokes run from R20 to the edge of each cage node.

Anatomy

Six named parts. Node radius and value both step with depth — nearest is largest and lightest, farthest smallest and deepest.

03 · Colourways

Five, and no others

The six dashed spokes carry #FF9933 — the reach between members. The depth ramp inverts between grounds: on light, near nodes go dark and orange steps down to #E0771A; on dark, near nodes go pale and orange stays #FF9933. One exception: on brand green the orange drops out entirely (it matches the ground’s luminance) and the knockout is single-value.

07 · Application

The identity in use

App icons
The primary tile is the dark ground: orange sits at almost exactly brand green’s luminance, so it cannot be shown on #009966. Where a green tile is required (last), the spokes revert to the pale knockout tint and the mark carries no orange. The mark fills 78% of the tile on its tall axis and 69.5% on its wide axis, measured on the artwork’s ink bounds (x 23.4–107.6, y 20–114.5) rather than its 130-unit artboard — iOS wants 75–80%, Android’s adaptive safe zone 66%. Centre on the ink box too: its centre is y 67.25, not the artboard’s 65, so artboard-centred placement sits visibly low. No gradient, no inner shadow; corner radius follows the platform.

Motion
One behaviour only: on load or on brand moments, the six spokes draw outward from the hub in sequence (60ms stagger, 240ms each, ease-out), and the cage nodes fade up as each spoke lands. Nothing rotates, nothing bounces, and the dash pattern never animates as a marching line.
